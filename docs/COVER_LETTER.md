# Cover letter: Devin Ambassador Program

Dear Cognition Community Team,

I'm applying to the Devin Ambassador Program because I believe powerful AI tools should be accessible to curious,
capable people from many backgrounds, not only people who followed a conventional path into software engineering.

I'm Duke, known online as DukeZak37. I'm an honorably discharged U.S. Army veteran and an automotive sales
professional. As a Fire Finder radar operator, I learned to work with sophisticated electronic
systems, troubleshoot under pressure, communicate clearly with a team, and put safety ahead of shortcuts. Equipment
and mechanical work and sales taught me that solutions have to work for real people.

Alongside my technical work, I bring real-world communication experience. I've presented to small groups and at
meetings, spoken publicly, and made videos online. From early voice-chat platforms such as Roger Wilco to Teams, Zoom,
AnyDesk, Discord and Twitch, I've learned to reach people using the tools they already understand.

I've spent thousands of hours, by my own estimate, experimenting with Grok on games, skill-driven workflows, bots and
agent systems. That curiosity grew into Third Mind, a human-directed project where specialized AI agents collaborate
while staying accountable to the person guiding them. I work closely with Devin through an engineering
agent we call Bahamut. Together we've built an agent hub with approval gates and an audit log (78 tests rerun
independently, 84 reported on the latest build), Skynet, a clearly labeled Discord bot in my community server, and a
defensive security-monitoring prototype. Every
result is labeled live, dev-tested, simulated or planned, and the AI replies in Discord are still marked simulated.

What I appreciate most about Devin is that it gives someone like me a real opportunity to turn a complicated vision
into structured engineering tasks and reviewable work. I'm grateful to Cognition for making that possible, and I truly
enjoy working with your agents, with the responsibility to stay honest about limitations, security and results.

As an ambassador, I'd like to share practical building stories, accessible tutorials and live demonstrations, with
patient walkthroughs, honest results and room for questions. I'd gladly host Q&A sessions if that's useful. I want to
help newcomers ask better questions, guide agents effectively, test what those agents produce, and build
communities where people can learn without being made to feel they don't belong. I'd also give detailed
product feedback as a highly engaged, nontraditional builder.

My goal is simple: help people and build better systems for everyone. Thank you for considering my application.

Sincerely,
Duke (DukeZak37)
